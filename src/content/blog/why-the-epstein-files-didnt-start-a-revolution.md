## The Overton Window

There's a concept that helps explain what's going on here called the Overton window. It's the range of ideas that a society considers acceptable, what feels normal to talk about or believe. Everything within the window is seen as reasonable, and what is outside it is not, to varying degrees from not very normal to radical to unthinkable. But that window isn't fixed. It can shift over time. And one of the ways it shifts is through repeated exposure to the extreme, the unthinkable. Not because it will be accepted by the masses, but because it makes the less extreme things seem more acceptable. And in the process of being repeatedly exposed to the unthinkable, the unthinkable itself becomes familiar, too. I believe there are four forces pushing that window in the United States towards a place where something like the Epstein files doesn't mobilize people, it overwhelms them.
