## Colonization and Resistance

## Climate Change and the Reindeer

"How is climate change impacting this part of the world?" "This winter started very well. We got cold conditions pretty fast, and that's what we want. We want cold weather for weeks before the snow comes, because if snow comes on warm ground it becomes an icy surface. And because the reindeer eat from the ground, if you get an icy surface they can't reach the food, or actually they can't smell the food, and if they can't smell the food they will not start digging, and eventually they will starve because they don't get any food."

"It's very interesting, because when I asked you about climate change, your first answer was how it impacts the reindeer."
