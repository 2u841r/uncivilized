"Can I interview you? I'm Palestinian, asking people why they support Palestine." "I'm a human being. This isn't about religion. So it's not about being Muslim, being Jewish, whatever. "And can I ask you, why are you here today in this march supporting Palestine? What does it mean to you?" "Above all, we're all humans, and we are witnesses. They're not going to show it to you, because the narrative is Palestinian lives." "And water. And I'm so proud to be here, because here it's not just Muslims, it's not just Asians. "You're American, yeah?" "American." "Why are you here supporting Palestine today?" "It's the most clear and obvious injustice going on in the world. There is one story: it is Israeli apartheid, and it is as simple as that."
