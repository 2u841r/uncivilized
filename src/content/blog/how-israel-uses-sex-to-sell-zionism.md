## The new Jew

This Jew would be rugged, muscular, strong, and tanned, and would have the mental and physical strength to realize the goals of Zionism and colonize Palestine.

They called themselves "sabra," the prickly pear fruit in Hebrew, to represent what they saw this new Jew to be: hard and prickly on the outside but tenderhearted and sensitive on the inside, and, of course, native to the land.
