Did you know about human zoos? Live human beings were put on display, where people were treated like animals and put on full display. According to psychologists, this is one of the five stages of dehumanization that often lead to the most horrific crimes and acts in human history. You're probably witnessing these five stages unfold all around us, from the torture and rape of Palestinian prisoners, to the targeting and abuse of the Algerian boxer, to the far-right racist and anti-immigrant violence in the UK. "Terrorist. Muslim terrorist." We use the word "dehumanization" all the time, but do we understand the psychology behind it?

## Stage One: The Lesser Human

The first stage is the lesser human. This is about labeling a group of people as less evolved human beings that either possess inferior intelligence or are more likely to be lazy, dirty, violent, childlike, deceitful, rudish, or corrupt. This is where stereotypes come from, and they often reflect hierarchies established by supremacist ideologies. Anything that challenges them is a threat to the natural order of things, like an Arab woman being strong and powerful rather than exotic, weak, submissive, and in need of saving. Establishing the lesser human is about making a distinction between those who are civilized and evolved and those that are uncivilized and primitive.

## Stage Two: Infestation

The second stage is infestation. Once established as lesser humans, the group of people are painted as a threat to the safety and wellbeing of the dominant group. This is most commonly done through analogies of infestation or plagues that aim to damage, destroy, or spread disease among a healthy, thriving society. And in what way do you usually deal with an infestation? Well, you call an exterminator, because you need to make something great again. But let's not get ahead of ourselves; that comes at a later stage.

## Stage Three: Animals

Stage three is animals. At this stage, people move from being lesser humans to becoming stripped of their humanity altogether; they become an entirely different species. There are many famous cases of that in history, like the Nazis referring to Jews as rats, Hutus calling Tutsis cockroaches, and Israelis outright calling Palestinians animals. All three of these examples were the precursors of genocides.

This psychologically lays the foundation for violence by excluding that group from the human moral and ethical code, and so almost anything goes. Once primed through these narratives, often pushed by politicians and the media, the dominant group begins normalizing violence. It is no surprise that we're seeing this play out across the UK following an election where racism and fascist ideas were stoked and became part of the normal public discourse. These conditions for violence are not random but carefully crafted and designed.

## Stage Five: Removal and Eradication

Stage five is removal and eradication. This final stage of dehumanization is the removal, eradication, and extermination of this group from society, because there's no other way to deal with this non-human that is also an existential threat to us. This unleashes horrific violence that we are now witnessing in Gaza, and the Israeli rape and torture camps.

Ethnic cleansing and genocide are a product of this very deliberate psychological process that strips humanity from people to offer a license to commit the unspeakable. These are happening all around us at different stages. Not only do we need to understand what they are, but also the intent behind where they want to take us, because stories and narratives matter. Because one day we can wake up and have an entire people almost no longer with us.
