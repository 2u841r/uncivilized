The majority indigenous Mayan population lives in extreme poverty, forced to work land they don't own. Meanwhile, the largest landowner in the entire country, along with owning the railroad, port, and telecommunications, is an American corporation, the United Fruit Company. Guatemalans refer to it as the octopus, its tentacles reaching every corner of not just Guatemala, but most of Central America. "For many years, these fascinating tropical countries have been served by United Fruit Company's Great White Fleet." On its plantations, workers toil under horrible conditions. Many are debt slaves, and poverty, illiteracy, and malnutrition are widespread. Then in 1951, Jacobo Arbenz comes to power with a mission to redistribute land to ensure the poor have their own, including hundreds of thousands of acres of unused land owned by the United Fruit Company. They were offered compensation, but that didn't matter. United Fruit would not allow this to continue. "No less a figure than John Foster Dulles, head of the State Department, was part of the firm of lawyers acting for the United Fruit Company. His brother Allen was the head of the CIA."
