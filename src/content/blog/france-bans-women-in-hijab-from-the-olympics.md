## A long history of bans

The hijab and Islam in general have long been a target of the French establishment. Back in 2019, a sports store tried to launch their first sports hijab and it caused hysteria in France. France has a long history of criminalizing being visibly Muslim in public places, all under the pretense that it threatens laïcité, the constitutional principle of secularism in France. It's banned the hijab in schools, public pools, and public institutions, and now many members of the parliament want to extend the ban to all sports competitions.

And it's only ramping up. The far-right Alternative for Germany party has come second in the European vote. The arch-conservative Brothers of Italy group won the most votes, with far-right anti-immigration nationalist politicians gaining more and more ground all over Europe, including in France. All this in a country home to millions of Muslims, both recent immigrants and French citizens who've lived there for generations.
